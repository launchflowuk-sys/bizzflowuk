Why I halved the budget

We had this set at £35 a day. I've brought it down to £18, and the reasoning is worth two minutes of your time.

At £35 a day your credit is gone in about three weeks. At £18 it lasts around six. That extra time is the whole point — the first few weeks of any campaign, Google shows your ads against a load of searches that sound right but aren't. People hunting colour charts, DIY guides, suppliers, students doing coursework. My job is to spot those and block them, one week at a time.

More weeks of credit means more of that cleaning done before you're spending your own money rather than after. Same ads, same targeting, just a slower burn and a sharper campaign at the end of it.

What to expect

Roughly seven visits a day to your quote pages. Once it settles down, that should land somewhere around two to five enquiries a week.

Two honest caveats

Week one is always the noisiest. Some of those early clicks will be junk — that's normal, and it's exactly what the first prune is for. Don't read anything into it.

And don't judge the campaign on a handful of clicks. Give it a month before either of us draws a conclusion. Anyone who tells you they can read a Google Ads campaign in three days is guessing.
