04 · The gapThree things costing you jobs

You have no proof, and proof is what sells.

I searched Google, Yell, Checkatrade, MyBuilder and RatedPeople for AMO Rendering. You appear on none of them. To a homeowner comparing three quotes, that reads as risk.

1
No Google Business Profile

This is the big one. It is free, it puts you on the map pack for “rendering near me”, and it is where reviews live. Without it you are invisible in the exact place people look first.

2
The site claims “5★ average” with no reviews behind it

A rating with nothing to click through to is worse than no rating — it invites doubt about everything else on the page. We either back it up or take it down.

3
A Grays business with an Edgware address in the footer

Every headline says Grays; the footer says Whitchurch Lane, Edgware. Bro-Therm, Essex Render Pros and Ultimate Plastering all show a local base. A careful customer notices.
