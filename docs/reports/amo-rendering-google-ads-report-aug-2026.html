1 · Executive summary

The launch period did its job — and told us exactly what to change

The first weeks of a new Google Ads campaign are a data-gathering phase: we let Google's auction show us which searches it matches, what they cost, and which of them turn into enquiries. That data is now in, and it was decisive.

The campaign attracted a healthy volume of attention — a 4.58% click-through rate is a strong result for a new account — but the searches that consumed the budget were dominated by product research (colour charts, render brands, materials suppliers) rather than homeowners looking to hire a renderer. Combined with an automated bidding strategy that was paying a premium per click, this produced traffic without enquiries.

We have now completed a full rebuild of the campaign — bidding, keywords, ad copy, campaign structure and call tracking — detailed in section 4. The campaign is currently paused at your request and is ready to relaunch on your go-ahead.

The problem this rebuild fixes

In plain terms: the ads were good enough to get clicked, but the campaign was paying too much per click for the wrong kinds of searches. Both issues are structural, both are now fixed.

3 · What the audit found

Four root causes, identified from the search data

1

Automated bidding was paying a premium

The campaign ran on Google's "Maximise Clicks" strategy, which ignores manual bid limits. Individual clicks cost up to £13, against a realistic local market rate of £2–3. On a £15/day budget, that meant only around two clicks per day.

2

The budget was matched to research searches, not hiring searches

Google's broad matching sent the ads to people researching render products, colour charts, suppliers and DIY methods — searches that will never become a rendering enquiry. Verified examples from Google's own search report are below.

3

Four of the six ads were serving with policy restrictions

Google restricts ads that include a phone number in the ad text. Four ads carried "Call 01375 506071" in their copy and were flagged "Approved (Limited)", which suppresses how often they are shown.

4

The highest-value local searches had no coverage

Searches such as "rendering companies near me" and "house rendering Essex" — the clearest hire-a-renderer intent there is — matched no keyword in the campaign. With very little competing advertising from other local rendering firms, these searches are the campaign's biggest untapped opportunity.

4 · Optimisation work completed

Full campaign rebuild — completed 5–6 August 2026

Bidding moved to manual cost control Cost

Replaced automated bidding with fixed cost-per-click ceilings: £2.50 across the service ad groups and £2.00 for render repairs. The £6.94 average — and the £13 outliers — cannot recur. At the capped rate, the same daily budget buys roughly three times as many clicks.

Around 70 new negative keywords added Targeting

Every wasted-spend category found in the search data is now blocked: colour charts, product brands (K Rend products, Weber, Kingspan, Rockwool and others), suppliers and merchants, DIY and "how to" searches, grant and free-scheme hunters, and price-calculator queries. The budget is reserved for people looking to hire.

All ad copy rewritten and policy-cleared Ads

Phone numbers were removed from ad text and every restricted ad rewritten and resubmitted, so ads can serve at full strength. The number is not lost — see the call button below.

New "House Rendering" ad group launched Growth

A dedicated ad group now targets the strongest local hiring searches — "rendering companies near me", "house rendering Essex", "renderers near me", "rendering quote" and similar — where local competition is minimal.

Call button added to all ads Leads

A Google-approved call asset for 01375 506071 is now attached account-wide, putting a tappable call button on mobile ads — the compliant (and more effective) replacement for a number in the ad text.

Enquiry tracking extended Measurement

Quote-form enquiries were already tracked; conversion tracking has now been extended to the website's cost-calculator leads as well, so every enquiry the ads generate is counted and attributed.

5 · What to expect next

Relaunch plan and reporting

The rebuilt campaign is paused and ready. On your approval we will re-enable it at the current £15/day budget. With clicks capped at £2.50 and the budget pointed at hiring-intent searches only, expect fewer — but far better — visitors: roughly five to six qualified clicks per day instead of two expensive, poorly-matched ones.
