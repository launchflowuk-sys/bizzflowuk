What I found

Rendering a house is a big spend on the front of somebody's home. People don't commit to that off a web form. They want to see work you've done, read what other customers said, and be satisfied you're a real firm based near them.

At the moment, someone clicking your ad lands on a page with no photos of any job you've completed, no reviews, no Google listing, and a company address in Edgware while the page tells them you're a Grays firm. The offer is fine. There's just nothing sitting behind it.

Google works it out too, and it charges you for it.

This is why your clicks are expensive. Google doesn't score you on trust directly, but the gaps show up three ways.

Without a Google Business listing, your ads can't show a location line. That makes the ad physically smaller on the page, so fewer people click it. Google uses your click rate to work out Ad Rank, and a weaker Ad Rank means you pay more to sit in the same position. Second, Google also scores the quality of the page people land on, and a thin page marks you down again. Third, you're not in the free map results at the top of local searches, which is where most "renderer near me" clicks go, so every single visitor has to be paid for.

Your first month averaged £6.94 a click. I've since capped the bids at £2.50 and cut out the rubbish searches. Getting your click rate up is the other half of that job, and that needs the listing, the photos and the reviews.

What I need from you

In order of what unblocks the most. The first three are holding money up right now.

Photos of finished jobs, before and after This week

This is the biggest single thing and it costs you an afternoon. You've just come off a run of work, so those houses are standing out there right now.

  • Send me everything already on your phone, however rough it looks. I'll pick what works.

Set up your Google Business listing This week

It's free, and right now it's the biggest hole in the whole setup. I'll sit down and do it with you if that's easier.

Important bit: we set it up as a service area business. That means it's registered at the address you actually work from in Grays, the address stays hidden from the public, and we list your service areas as Grays, Thurrock, Basildon, Tilbury and the surrounding patch.

You don't need to rent an office in Grays. Service area is the correct setup for a trade that travels to the customer, and it's what every decent local firm uses.

Verification is normally a postcard or a video call and can take a week or two, which is why I want it started now rather than after everything else.

What it unlocks: location details on your ads, which means a bigger ad, a better click rate and a lower cost per click. Plus a free listing in the local map results, and somewhere for your reviews to actually live.
