1 · Summary

Launch phase complete — campaign upgraded and relaunched

The first weeks of any new Google Ads campaign are a learning phase: Google's data shows us exactly which searches local people make, what they cost, and which are most likely to become enquiries. We've now used that data to complete a full round of optimisation across the whole campaign.

The early signals are encouraging — your ads earned a 4.58% click-through rate, a strong result for a brand-new campaign that tells us the ads and the offer resonate with local searchers. The upgrades below sharpen who sees those ads and lock in the price paid for every visit, so the budget works harder from here on.

3 · Optimisations completed

Six upgrades, applied 5–6 August 2026

Guaranteed value per click Cost control

Bidding moved to manual cost control with a fixed ceiling of £2.50 per click (£2.00 for repair searches). Every click now comes at a known, capped price — the budget stretches further and spend is fully predictable.

Laser-focused targeting Targeting

Around 70 new negative keywords now filter out searches that can't become enquiries — product research, colour charts, suppliers, DIY guides and trade queries. Your budget is reserved exclusively for homeowners looking to hire.

Fresh ad copy across every service Ads

All ads rewritten to Google's current best practice — sharper headlines, clear guarantees (15-year silicone guarantee, free 24-hour quotes) and full compliance, so they serve at maximum strength.

New "House Rendering" campaign arm Growth

A dedicated ad group now targets the highest-value local searches — "rendering companies near me", "house rendering Essex", "renderers near me" and similar. These are the clearest hire-a-renderer searches there are, and local competition on them is minimal.

Tap-to-call on mobile ads Leads

A call button for 01375 506071 is now attached to your ads, so mobile searchers can ring you directly from the search results — one tap, no website needed.

Every enquiry now measured Measurement

Conversion tracking has been extended so both quote-form enquiries and cost-calculator enquiries are counted and attributed to the ads — you'll always know exactly what the campaign is producing.

4 · What happens next

Relaunched and monitored weekly

The upgraded campaign went live on 6 August at the agreed £15/day budget. With clicks capped at £2.50 and targeting focused on hiring intent, expect a steady flow of well-qualified local visitors rather than a high volume of casual ones.
