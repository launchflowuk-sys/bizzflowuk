What survived, and what didn't

Recoverable in full

All of it is in the CSVs below.

Not recoverable

  • Billing setup and account history. Rebuild from scratch.

Nothing was serving anyway — all 7 ads were paused, so no live spend or lead flow was lost to the deactivation itself.

02

Check this today, before the appeal resolves

Is the Google Business Profile on the same account?

AMO Rendering's listing was verified only recently. If amorendering@gmail.com owns or manages that profile, the deactivation may have taken the listing with it — and for a local trade, a verified GBP is worth more than the ad account.

Check now whether the listing still appears in Search and Maps, and whether any other email has manager access. If it doesn't, add a second owner from a different account the moment access returns. A profile with one owner on one Gmail is a single point of failure.

Also worth confirming: whether the Ads account itself sat under launchflowuk@gmail.com rather than the deactivated address. The Supermetrics connection ran through launchflowuk@gmail.com, so account access may be less damaged than it looks. That is worth five minutes before assuming a full rebuild.

03

If the appeal succeeds

Nothing here needs to run. Go straight back to the outstanding job: the 7 ads are approved but paused, and the campaign cannot serve until they are enabled in the Ads UI — Campaign → Ads → select all → Edit → Enable. Do not edit them through the API; any edit recreates an ad as paused and wipes its history.

04

If the appeal fails — rebuild

  1. Create the new Google Ads account on the new email. Set billing.
  2. Create one Search campaign named AMO - Rendering Services with the settings in the table below. Set it up paused.
  3. Open Google Ads Editor, get the new account, then import in this order: keywords first, then the responsive search ads. Review the proposed-changes screen before posting — it is the only preview you get.
  4. Apply negative-keywords.csv as a campaign-level list before anything goes live.
  5. Recreate the conversion action ("Quote Request Submitted"). Copy the new conversion ID and label.
  6. Put those into BizzFlow: dashboard → Settings → googleAdsConversionId and googleAdsConversionLabel for the AMO Rendering tenant. Until this is done the campaign cannot measure a single lead.
  7. Add the call asset with the existing approved number, 01375 506071, at account level. Keep phone numbers out of ad text — that is what put four ads into limited approval on the original build.
  8. Fire one real test enquiry through /free-quote and confirm the conversion records in Ads, not just GA4. Only then enable.
